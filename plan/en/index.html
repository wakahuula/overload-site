Plan format overload-plan v1

Overload reads training plans as JSON. A plan gets into the app as a file someone shared from Overload (Name.overload.json) or as pasted text, such as an AI's answer. Overload contains no AI and sends nothing anywhere.

In the app, Plans › Import › Plan with AI writes instructions for your own AI. Its answer comes back via Paste plan. Overload checks every field and shows what arrives before saving.

Machine-readable: JSON Schema (draft 2020-12).

Structure

Each workout is written once, under a key in workouts. The weeks in weeks are lists of days pointing to those keys. null is a rest day. If the sequence of weeks repeats, write it once and set repeat to how often it runs. A twelve-week plan stays short, and weeks can still differ.

Top level

FieldRule
formatrequired, exactly "overload-plan"
versionrequired, exactly 1. The app rejects a higher version and asks for an update.
namerequired, 1 to 80 characters
descriptionoptional, up to 500 characters
authoroptional, up to 80 characters
styleoptional: bodybuilding, powerbuilding, powerlifting, strongman, crossfit, calisthenics, general_fitness
leveloptional: beginner, intermediate, advanced
unitoptional, kg or lbs, default kg. Applies to every weight in the plan.
workoutsrequired, 1 to 14 workouts. Keys of 1 to 32 characters from a-z, 0-9, _ and -. Each workout has name and exercises (1 to 20 exercises).
weeksrequired, 1 to 52 weeks
repeatoptional, 1 to 12, default 1. Total weeks (weeks times repeat) at most 52.

Only in plans Overload shares itself: origin (lineage for update and duplicate detection), photo (data URI, JPEG, PNG or WebP) and customExercises (custom exercises without a catalog slug).

Exercise

FieldRule
exerciserequired. A slug from the app's catalog, such as bench-press, or a name. Overload creates unknown names as custom exercises and marks them in the preview.
setsrequired, whole number 1 to 20
reps / seconds / metersexactly one of them. Whole number or [min, max]. Reps 1 to 100, seconds 1 to 3600, meters 1 to 10000.
restrest in seconds, 0 to 900, default 120
loadoptional, exactly one of: weight (in unit), percentTM (1 to 120, percent of training max, only with reps), rpe (5 to 10 in half steps)
progressionoptional, see below
amrapLastoptional, true: last set as many reps as possible
supersetoptional, label up to 8 characters. Same label means superset.
notesoptional, up to 500 characters

Progression

progression has a rule and that rule's parameters. Weight steps are in the plan's unit.

RuleParameters
linearstep: more weight after every completed session
doubleupperReps, step: reps first up to upperReps, then more weight
amrapstep: increase based on the last set taken to as many reps as possible
rpetargetRpe, tolerance
rirtargetRir, tolerance
percentagestepPercent, percentBase (tm or weight), only with reps
cyclecycleSteps, cycleReps: one value per cycle week
timestepSeconds, maxSeconds
distancestepDistance, maxDistance
noneno increase

Week

Each week has days with 1 to 7 entries: a workout key or null for a rest day. "deload": true marks a deload week.

How Overload checks

  • Text up to 2 MB. If there is text before or after the JSON, Overload takes the first code block, or everything from the first { to the last }.
  • Unknown fields are errors, not silently dropped. Otherwise nobody would notice "weight_kg" written instead of "load".
  • Cross-checks: every key in days exists under workouts, every workout appears in at least one week, and the plan has at least one training day.
  • Errors are collected with their path, for example workouts.upper.exercises[2].sets: must be a whole number from 1 to 20 (found: "4-5"). "Copy errors" hands them back to the AI with a short request.
